Supply and shortage designations

Clinicians are individual NPPES registrations by practice address, not full-time equivalents. Hospitals are active acute-care and critical-access hospitals in the CMS Provider of Services file (Q3 2026), with certified beds. Shortage areas are HRSA geographic and population HPSAs, designated or proposed for withdrawal; the share is the designated population divided by the state's 2020 population.

Method and caveats

  • Providers: NPPES November 2026, active NPIs, grouped by primary taxonomy exactly as on the Vermont map. About 92% of addresses matched a street address in the Census geocoder; the rest use the ZIP centroid.
  • Roads: OpenStreetMap extracts for VT, NH, MA, NY, ME, CT and RI, typical speeds by road class, slower on unpaved roads, no traffic, ferries or seasonal closures. Lake Champlain is a barrier except at its bridges.
  • Population: 2020 Census blocks (DHC). Statistics are computed per block; the map shows 1.5 km squares.
  • Not included: Québec, providers more than 30 miles outside the three states, and anything about capacity (hours, new patients, insurance accepted). A provider nearby is not the same as an appointment available.
