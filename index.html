Notes

Drive times use typical speeds by road class (slower on unpaved roads), with no traffic or winter closures (e.g. Smugglers' Notch, Route 108). Each 1.5 km grid square is timed from its centre, plus a short off-road leg to the nearest road. Only Vermont roads and providers are included, so border areas may have closer care in NH, NY, MA or Québec. Locations are NPPES practice addresses, which are sometimes billing offices. Hollow dots are placed at a ZIP centroid. Population is from the 2020 Census (DHC) by block, assigned to the grid square containing each block's centre; small-block counts include Census privacy noise, but totals are reliable.
