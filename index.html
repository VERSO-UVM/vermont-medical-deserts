Notes

Drive times use typical speeds by road class (slower on unpaved roads), with no traffic or seasonal closures. Only Vermont roads and providers are included, so border towns may have closer care in NH, NY, MA or Québec.

Provider locations are NPPES practice addresses, sometimes billing offices. Hollow dots sit at a ZIP centroid. "Billed Medicare" keeps clinicians with Medicare Part B claims in their own name in 2024; it is only offered where that data is meaningful.

Safety-net clinics are HRSA health center sites and CMS-enrolled rural health clinics. School-based and nursing-home sites (hollow) serve only their own students or residents and are not used for drive times.

Population: 2020 Census blocks. Households without a vehicle: ACS 2020–2024 by block group, spread over its blocks by population. Shortage areas: HRSA, designated or proposed for withdrawal.
